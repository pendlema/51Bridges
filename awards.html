2025 Indiana Rural Small Business of the Year

Indiana Economic Development Corporation

More Than a Gym – A Community Inspiring Health and Wellness

We are honored and humbled to be named the 2025 Indiana Rural Small Business of the Year by the Indiana Economic Development Corporation (IEDC) and Governor Mike Braun. This recognition celebrates the impact that small businesses have on Indiana's economy and communities.


After transforming their own lives through CrossFit, Jennifer and Mike Pendleton founded 51 Bridges CrossFit in Rockville to help others do the same. What started as a personal journey of health transformation has grown into a thriving community hub that inspires wellness and changes lives.


Our facility offers inclusive programs for all fitness levels, emphasizing functional movements and high-intensity workouts. But more importantly, we've built a supportive community where everyone—from first-time gym-goers to experienced athletes—feels welcomed, encouraged, and empowered to reach their goals.

About the Award

Presented during Indiana Small Business Week (May 5-9, 2025), this award recognizes small businesses that have worked with the Indiana Small Business Development Center (SBDC) to start, grow, or pivot their operations while making a significant impact on their communities.

👥

Community Impact

51 Bridges CrossFit has become a cornerstone of health and wellness in Parke County, bringing people together and transforming lives through fitness.
