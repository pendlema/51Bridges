CrossFit Open

What is the CrossFit Open?

The CrossFit Open is the world's largest participatory sporting event, bringing together hundreds of thousands of athletes from around the globe. For three weeks, athletes of all levels complete challenging workouts and submit their scores to see how they stack up against the worldwide CrossFit community.


At 51 Bridges CrossFit, the Open is more than just competition—it's a celebration of our community. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or trying your first Open workout, we come together to push ourselves, support each other, and have fun!

Why Participate?

  • Test Your Fitness

    The Open workouts challenge you in new ways and give you a benchmark to measure your progress year after year.

  • Community Spirit

    Join athletes worldwide in this shared experience. At our gym, we'll have Open workout sessions where we tackle the workouts together.

  • Push Your Limits

    The Open motivates you to try movements and weights you might not attempt in a regular class.

  • All Levels Welcome

    Scaled and modified divisions mean everyone can participate regardless of experience or ability.
