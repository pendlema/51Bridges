Welcome to 51 Bridges CrossFit

Located just 2 blocks off the square in historic downtown Rockville, Indiana, our facility is designed to provide everything you need for functional fitness training. With 5,000 square feet of training space, top-quality equipment, and a welcoming atmosphere, 51 Bridges CrossFit is where community meets fitness.


We're conveniently located 15 minutes from Turkey Run State Park and Raccoon Lake State Recreation Area, making us the perfect stop for travelers and locals alike. Come see why our members call this place home!
