Kids

CrossFit Kids is a 4-week course held periodically through the year. We incorporate functional movements in a fun, energetic way with workouts, relays, and games for kids age 5-12.


Parents can reach out to us about their 13-year olds to determine if it is more appropriate for them to join the Kids course or regular CrossFit. We’d be happy to talk it through or schedule an orientation/assessment.


Registration information is posted on our Facebook page two weeks before each session begins.
