What is CrossFit

CrossFit is a complete fitness experience that has the ability to redefine you. With over 10,000 affiliated gyms across 150 countries, 51 Bridges CrossFit in Parke County is at the center of the world’s largest and most influential fitness movement. We’re here to fight the battle against chronic disease through functional fitness and eating real food. Big fitness and real health is right here in our small town.


CrossFit is a one hour class combining functional movements, strength training, and cardio in dynamic group. Guided by professional coaching (and nutritional support if you’re looking for it), CrossFit delivers unmatched fitness results you can see and feel. At 51B we are athletes of all ages, abilities, shapes and sizes who come together to train, grow, and thrive as a community. If you’re looking to better your health, get stronger or if you are a fire-breather who wants to Forge Elite Fitness, our programming is for you.

Why CrossFit

  • Expert Coaches

    Every class is led by professionals trained to teach proper technique, safety, and effective progressions. Mechanics always come first, then consistency in those mechanics, then and only then will your coach encourage you to load.

  • Constantly Varied

    Every workout is unique, keeping things fresh and helping you avoid plateaus.

  • Designed for Results

    Our programming is science-backed and delivers power, fitness, and with consistency, noticeable body-composition changes.

  • Community

    CrossFit stands out because of its vibrant, welcoming community. Imagine training in an environment where everyone cheers you on and celebrates your progress. The supportive atmosphere and genuine camaraderie keep you motivated, accountable, and inspired (by yourself and others).

  • Best Hour of your Day

    Our one hour classes pack a punch, complete with a brief about the WOD (workout of the day), warm up, workout and cool down. Some days there will be a strength or skill session added as well. Our goal is to make it the best hour of your day!
