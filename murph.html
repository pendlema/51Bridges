Memorial Day Murph

Honoring Lt. Michael Murphy

Every Memorial Day, CrossFit gyms around the world come together to honor fallen heroes through the "Murph" workout. Named after Navy Lieutenant Michael Murphy, who was killed in action in Afghanistan in 2005, this challenging workout embodies the sacrifice and dedication of our service members.


The Murph workout consists of a 1-mile run, 100 pull-ups, 200 push-ups, 300 air squats, and another 1-mile run—typically done while wearing a 20-pound vest. At 51 Bridges CrossFit, we invite athletes of all abilities to participate in this meaningful tradition.

Why We Do Murph

  • Remember and Honor

    This workout is a powerful way to remember Lt. Murphy and all those who have given their lives in service to our country.

  • Challenge Yourself

    Murph is one of the most iconic CrossFit workouts, testing your endurance, mental toughness, and determination.

  • Community Unity

    Completing Murph together strengthens our gym community and connects us with the global CrossFit family.

  • All Abilities Welcome

    We offer scaled versions so everyone can participate and pay their respects, regardless of fitness level.
