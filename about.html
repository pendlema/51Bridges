Welcome to 51Bridges CrossFit

Our Story

Founded by Mike and Jen Pendleton, 51Bridges CrossFit started with a vision to create a community-centered fitness environment. After years of personal fitness experiences and insights, Mike and Jen realized the importance of building a supportive and inclusive atmosphere where individuals of all fitness levels could thrive. Their journey showcases their passion for wellness, strength, and community, creating a home for fitness enthusiasts in Rockville, Indiana.

Mission Statement

At 51Bridges CrossFit, our mission is to inspire and empower individuals to achieve their fitness goals through community support, expert coaching, and innovative training methods. We believe in the importance of health and wellness, striving to build a stronger, more connected community.

Why Choose 51Bridges?

  • Inclusive Community: We welcome individuals of all backgrounds and fitness levels.
  • Expert Coaching: Our certified trainers are dedicated to providing personalized guidance tailored to your needs.
  • Wide Range of Classes: From beginners to advanced athletes, we offer varied classes that cater to all fitness preferences.

Meet Our Coaching Staff

Our coaching staff in Rockville, Indiana, is comprised of experienced fitness professionals who are passionate about helping you achieve your personal best. Each coach brings unique skills and expertise, ensuring you have the support you need on your fitness journey.
