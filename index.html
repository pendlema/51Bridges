Welcome to 51 Bridges CrossFit

More Than a Gym – A Community Inspiring Health and Wellness

We are a functional fitness gym located 2 blocks off the square in Rockville, Indiana. Founded in CrossFit methodology, we welcome all fitness abilities—even if you've been out of the game for a while. We've built a supportive and fun community that will make your fitness journey a meaningful one you want to stick with.


After transforming their own lives through CrossFit, Jennifer and Mike Pendleton founded 51 Bridges CrossFit to help others do the same. Together, they lost over 200 pounds and got off all their medications. Now they're here to help you get on a path to strength, health, and wellness.

How to Get Started

We make it easy to begin your fitness journey

1

Schedule a No-Sweat Intro

Meet with owner and head coach Jen Pendleton for a free consultation. She understands how hard it can be to start and will help you feel comfortable.

2

Try a Free Orientation Class

Learn about CrossFit, our gym, and how everything works. The class ends with a 10-minute all-abilities workout.

3

Get 2 Free Classes

After orientation, you get two free classes to experience our programming and community before committing to a membership.

4

Join the Community

Choose a membership that fits your goals and become part of the 51 Bridges family. Your transformation starts here!
