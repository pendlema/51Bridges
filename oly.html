Olympic Weightlifting

A core of CrossFit is Olympic Weightlifting - the Clean and Jerk and the Snatch. 51B has a special barbell class three times a month on Saturday where you can learn from USA Weightlifting experts in a class setting. All abilities, including beginners are welcome.


CrossFit incorporates Olympic weightlifting in its programming to develop elite power, strength, speed, and coordination. These movements produce the highest power output of any exercise. These technical, full-body lifts enhance functional, real-world athleticism, improve neuromuscular control, and boost overall work capacity.


It is safe for all ages and abilities to learn. Our teaching progressions begin with PVC pipe and trainer barbells.
