Veterans Day Chad 1000X

Honoring Navy SEAL Chad Wilkinson

On Veterans Day, we honor all who have served our country by completing the "Chad 1000X" workout. This Hero WOD is dedicated to Navy SEAL Chad Wilkinson, who was killed in action in Afghanistan in 2018. The workout consists of 1,000 box step-ups, the same movement Chad performed in his final workout before his last deployment.


Chad 1000X is a powerful tribute that brings our community together in remembrance and gratitude. While challenging, this workout is accessible to all fitness levels and serves as a meaningful way to honor our veterans and fallen heroes.

Why Chad 1000X?

  • Remember and Honor

    This workout is a tribute to Chad Wilkinson and all veterans who have served and sacrificed for our freedom.

  • Mental Fortitude

    1,000 step-ups tests your mental toughness and determination, giving you a glimpse into the resilience of our service members.

  • Inclusive Challenge

    This workout can be scaled to any fitness level, allowing everyone to participate in honoring our veterans.

  • Community Support

    Complete this workout alongside your fellow athletes, encouraging each other through every step.
