CrossFit Community Cup

Celebrate Community Through Competition

The CrossFit Community Cup is our signature team competition that brings together athletes of all levels for a day of challenging workouts, teamwork, and community spirit. This event embodies what CrossFit is all about—pushing your limits alongside your friends and celebrating each other's achievements.


Teams of athletes work together to tackle a series of diverse workouts designed to test strength, endurance, skill, and strategy. Whether you're a competitive athlete or just looking for a fun challenge with your gym buddies, the Community Cup has a division for you.

Event Highlights

  • Team Competition

    Form teams with your fellow athletes and compete in multiple divisions based on skill level.

  • Varied Workouts

    Each heat brings new challenges, from heavy lifting to gymnastics to metabolic conditioning.

  • Community Focus

    This isn't just about winning—it's about bringing our community together for an unforgettable day of fitness and fun.

  • Spectator Friendly

    Bring your family and friends to watch, cheer, and see what CrossFit is all about!
