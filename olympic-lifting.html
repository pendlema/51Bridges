Olympic Lifting

Welcome to the Olympic lifting section of 51Bridges CrossFit! Olympic lifting is a highly technical and physically demanding discipline that involves two main lifts: the Snatch and the Clean and Jerk. Our experienced coaches guide athletes through the fundamentals and intricacies of these lifts to maximize performance and minimize injury risk.

Benefits of Olympic Lifting

  • Improved strength and power
  • Enhanced athletic performance
  • Increased flexibility and mobility
  • Engagement of multiple muscle groups

Join Our Classes

At 51Bridges CrossFit, we offer specialized Olympic lifting classes suitable for all levels. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned athlete, you can benefit from our tailored coaching. Come join us and lift with us!
