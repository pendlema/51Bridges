Lift Local

24 Hours for Veterans

Supporting Our Local Veterans

Lift Local is 51 Bridges CrossFit's annual 24-hour fundraising event benefiting the Parke County Veterans Council. From noon to noon, our community comes together to complete partner Hero WODs (workouts of the day), each one honoring a fallen service member. This incredible event combines fitness, community, and service to support those who have served our country.


Held every summer at our gym in Rockville, Indiana, Lift Local brings together experienced CrossFitters of all levels for a day (and night!) of challenging workouts, camaraderie, and giving back. Whether you participate in one workout or stay for several, you'll be part of something truly special while raising funds for veterans in Parke County.

Event Details

  • 24 Hours of Hero WODs

    From Friday noon to Saturday noon, we complete one partner Hero WOD every hour, honoring fallen heroes and supporting our local veterans.

  • Partner Teams

    All workouts are done in teams of 2 or 3. Don't have a partner? No problem—we'll pair you with others at the event.

  • Flexible Participation

    Sign up for 1-2 WODs or tackle several throughout the 24 hours. Stay and cheer on others or come back multiple times—it's up to you!

  • Making an Impact

    The suggested donation is $20 per WOD, with 100% of donations going directly to the Parke County Veterans Council. Past events have featured matching programs that triple donations!

  • Sponsorship Opportunities

    Businesses, families, and individuals can sponsor the event with options from hourly sponsorships to title sponsorships. Contact us to learn more.

  • All Levels Welcome

    Experienced CrossFitters from beginners to advanced are encouraged to participate. Coaches provide equipment setup and scaling options, so choose WODs with movements you can execute safely.
